About Our Club

A community built around the beautiful game

Founded in 1980, Ballygawley Celtic FC has been the home of football in Ballygawley, Co. Sligo for over four decades. Our volunteers, parents, and coaches keep this club running because they believe football brings people together.

We field teams from U6 to U18, plus our Men's Senior side currently competing in the Sligo Leitrim & District Soccer League - Premier League after promotion in 25/26. We play at Ballygawley Community Park, where players train and compete in the spirit of fair play and development.

We hold FAI Club Mark accreditation, which recognises our standards in governance, coaching, child safeguarding, and community work.
